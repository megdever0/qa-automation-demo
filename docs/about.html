Background

I’m a Quality Assurance Engineer based in NYC with a little over 3 years of experience. I have a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science. During college, I worked as a consultant in the campus library computer labs, helping students and staff with tech issues. After graduating, I completed a DevOps/Software Engineering internship, and then I started my current role as a QA Engineer.

In my role, I mostly focus on manual testing of web applications. I create test plans, find bugs, collaborate with developers and product teams to solve problems, guide testing strategy, and refine requirements. Over the last year, I’ve started learning Playwright for automated testing. I’ve built test suites that save time on regression testing and show the power of automation in QA.

  • Automated testing with Playwright
  • Java, Python, SQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, C++
  • Collaborating in Agile teams
  • Reporting bugs, identifying edge cases and improving software quality
  • Jira, Postman, Tableau, Power BI, Microsoft Excel

Why I Started This Portfolio

I created this portfolio to practice and strengthen my programming and automation skills. My goal is to build webpages that I can use to run Playwright test cases, while also showcasing my work and learning in a hands-on way. I want to continue improving my automation skills and exploring how QA can make a real impact on software quality.
